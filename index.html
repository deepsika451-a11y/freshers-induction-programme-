About Programme

The Freshers' Induction Programme helps new students settle into college life, meet faculty and seniors, and learn about college rules and facilities.

Objectives

  1. Welcome First Year Students
  2. Introduce Departments And Faculty
  3. Explain Rules And Facilities
  4. Help Students Make New Friends
